# Rojak-Lingo datasheet

## Gold set (`data/gold/`)
- Hand-written test items for four tasks: normalize, intent, instruct, translate.
- Languages: English, Bahasa Malaysia, Mandarin (Simplified), Cantonese, Manglish.
- Every item has `status`: `draft` until the author reads and corrects it, then `verified`.
- The gold set is never used for training and never shown to the data-generating model.
- Reference answers are single references; chrF against one reference undercounts valid paraphrases.

## Synthetic training data (`data/synthetic/`, not committed)
- Generated by a teacher model from topic, language-mix and register seeds only.
- Instruct examples are kept only if the answer passes its own programmatic checks.
- Exact and near duplicates removed; n-gram overlap with gold checked by `make check`.
- Known limits: teacher output can sound synthetic; dialect and spelling coverage follows the teacher.

## Not included
- No scraped social media posts, chat logs or personal data.
